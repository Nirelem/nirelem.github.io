bio: Margarita Beatrice E. Raynera is a fourth-year undergraduate student from the BFA Creative Writing program of the Ateneo de Manila University. She was the sole awardee in the English category of the 2026 F. Sionil Jose Young Writers Awards, and was a Fellow for Fiction in the 1st Philippines Graphic Literary Workshop. She is also one of the featured contributors for the forthcoming anthology, "Monsters in Philippine Literature and Visual Arts.”
