bio: Hans Corral does not know what to write about herself, because she spent the better part of her university journey writing academically. She wishes to one day read a proper book again so she can veritably tell others her hobby is “reading,” but for now she settles on rediscovering how crashouts make good poetry. This gives her an outlet when she starts teaching—state of education in the Philippines, the one thing more abysmal than her reading habits. Oh wait, politics. There’s that.
