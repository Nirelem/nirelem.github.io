**Trigger Warnings:** mentions of death

I **c**ame across your story, love, 
The one y**o**u couldn’t tell.

Others, danger **n**ear, would scatter, 
(As one sho**u**ld with a warning shot) 
But no, not you; no, you would gathe**r** 
‘R**o**und the body’s morning rot

Did you **p**eek there, not lifelessness, 
But life **s**o full, refined? 
Or wh**i**ff the tangy faithfulness 
Of fruit**s** they often dined?

Was in that startled, haunted **c**ry 
Specks of wh**a**t could have been? 
Did thei**r** breeze, tearing through the sky, 
Still s**o**othe your fevered skin?

What was it **l**ike, if you may share, 
To “bravely” st**i**r and stand 
**N**ext to past and future, where 
Poor pr**e**sent can’t extend?

O**n**e still heart becomes many— 
What do brain**s** like ours have left to do? 
So there, k**i**n-feather, tell me: 
Did they weaponize your feeling**s** too?

—*to Conuropsis carolinensis, and all the birdbrains in a heartless system*
